## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an authoritative, high-clarity environment for workforce analytics, talent telemetry, and career path forecasting. It addresses mid-career professionals, enterprise mobility leaders, and talent strategists who require decisive, actionable data rather than superficial metrics.

The visual tone merges institutional trust with forward-looking intelligence. Drawing heavily from contemporary fintech precision and lucid glassmorphism, the interface communicates structural rigor, surgical accuracy, and high cognitive speed. Interactions feel low-friction, immediate, and mathematically organized. The emotional takeaway is one of mastery, transparency, and strategic momentum—transforming ambiguous skill taxonomies into deterministic career outcomes.

## Colors
The system relies on an immaculate light-mode canvas to maximize legibility and telemetry density.

- **Canvas & Layering**: The primary foundation is `#F8FAFC`, stepping to `#F1F5F9` for secondary structural wells and `#FFFFFF` for elevated telemetry cards, popovers, and interactive modules.
- **Accents & AI Semantics**: 
  - The primary electric indigo spectrum (`#4F46E5` core, `#6366F1` interactive/hover) drives core action targets, active navigation states, and systemic AI recommendations.
  - The cyber cyan/sky axis (`#0284C7` / `#0EA5E9`) represents secondary telemetry, comparative projections, and vector matching flows.
  - Emerald (`#059669`) strictly designates verified proficiencies, competitive advantages, and target readiness milestones.
  - Crimson/Rose (`#E11D48`) flags critical skill deficits, misalignments, and urgent market-demand gaps.
- **Borders & Dividers**: High-precision boundary separation uses `#E2E8F0` for card outlines and subtle sub-dividers, darkening to `#CBD5E1` for active interactive frames and structured data grids.
- **Typography & Hierarchy**: Primary headings utilize high-density deep midnight slate (`#0F172A`), body copy maintains strict contrast at `#334155`, and supporting metrics or timestamps reside at `#64748B`.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the universal type engine, leveraging its wide stance, contemporary geometric clarity, and high legibility across dense dashboard configurations.

- **Display & Headings**: Negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) tightens large titles, preventing sprawl and projecting executive composure.
- **Body & Data**: Normal letter tracking with generous line-height ratios guarantees continuous reading comfort across competency matrices and algorithmic career narratives.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Uppercase applications on `label-sm` employ increased letter-spacing (`0.04em`) to establish crisp section dividing anchors.
- **Telemetry Figures**: Scores, probabilities, salary bands, and percentile distributions reference JetBrains Mono via `mono-telemetry` to align tabular values and accentuate systematic calculation.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements a structural 12-column fluid grid system optimized for high-density information displays:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `gutter` at `1.5rem` (24px) and outer viewport padding anchored by `margin` at `2rem` (32px). Maximum container width clamps at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Complex charts collapse into 4-column pairing stacks.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4 columns with `gutter-mobile` at `1rem` (16px) and `margin-mobile` at `1rem` (16px). All parallel metric grids collapse into linear vertical sequences.

The spacing scale is derived from a strict 4px base. Interior card spacing standardizes on `space-md` (16px) for compact telemetry cards and `space-lg` (24px) for expansive analytical boards. Cross-section structural stacking leverages `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced via lucid glassmorphism balanced against fine-gauge vector boundaries:

- **Surface Translucency**: Primary floating elements and navigation overlays employ `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` surface fills paired with a `12px` to `16px` backdrop filter blur. This lets ambient canvas gradients peek through without sacrificing reading contrast.
- **Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are strictly low-opacity and tinted with deep slate (`#0F172A`) to avoid dirty gray halos:
  - *Tier 0 (Base Cards)*: No shadow. Rely entirely on a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`).
  - *Tier 1 (Elevated Cards & Hover)*: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`, bounded by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
  - *Tier 2 (Popovers, Select Menus, Flyouts)*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`, framed with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`).
  - *Tier 3 (Modals & Command Palettes)*: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
The design system deploys balanced geometric forms (Level 2):

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, control elements) use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Structural containers (cards, data tables, modals, callouts) utilize `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px).
- Badges, status pills, and skill chips apply complete pill radii (`9999px`) to visually isolate individual categorical tags from rectangular data frames.
- Segmented controls and toolbars wrap within `0.75rem` (12px) perimeter frames containing nested `0.5rem` active state targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#4F46E5` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` corner radius, `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` drop shadow. On hover, background shifts to `#6366F1`.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#94A3B8`.
- **AI Direct Action**: Subtle gradient from `#4F46E5` to `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, with a 1px inset border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent fill, `#334155` text. On hover, fills with `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.8)`.

### Skill Badges & Chips
- **Verified/Proficient**: Pill shape, background `#ECFDF5`, 1px border `#A7F3D0`, text `#059669`. Prepended with an emerald verified check icon.
- **Skill Gap/Deficit**: Pill shape, background `#FFF1F2`, 1px border `#FECDD3`, text `#E11D48`.
- **In-Progress/Target**: Pill shape, background `#F0F9FF`, 1px border `#BAE6FD`, text `#0284C7`.
- **AI Recommendation Tag**: Pill shape, background `#EEF2FF`, 1px border `#C7D2FE`, text `#4F46E5`. Includes an electric indigo spark glyph.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, typography `body-md` (`#0F172A`), placeholder text `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State**: Border switches to `#4F46E5` accompanied by an electric indigo focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.
- **Error State**: Border shifts to `#E11D48` with a ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.12)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: 1px border in `#CBD5E1` on `#FFFFFF` base. 
- Active: Fill `#4F46E5` with `#FFFFFF` glyph. Radio forms utilize a 4px inset white dot.

### Telemetry & Career Cards
- Background is `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `12px` backdrop blur over subtle slate canvas gradients. 1px outer stroke of `#E2E8F0`, rounded to `1rem` (16px).
- Internal layout groups key figures using `mono-telemetry` alongside clear categorical `label-sm` tags in `#64748B`.

### Trajectory / Path Progress Bars
- 6px height tracks with background `#E2E8F0` and `9999px` radius.
- Active fill: Solid `#059669` for validated progress, `#4F46E5` for projected trajectory, or banded segments highlighting delta gaps in `#E11D48`.
